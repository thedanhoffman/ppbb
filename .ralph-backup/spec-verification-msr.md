## Goal: Verify and correct all MSR/hardware implementations against spec documents

The power-balance daemon directly reads/writes MSRs and hardware registers. Since MSRs are model-specific and can vary between Intel platforms, we must cross-reference every implementation against the spec documents in `specs/` to ensure correctness.
